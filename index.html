Submit

Submit levels and records to the Wavelist

Sign in required Please sign in or create an account to submit records and levels to the list

Before You Submit

Please note before submitting a record that your record video must be uploaded to YouTube. Your video may be set to unlisted if you would like.

Record Requirements

  1. Must contain audible clicks, even on mobile
  2. Must contain your full unedited run, including your reaction and at least 3 attempts prior to your successful run
  3. Must show the end screen after level completion
  4. Must not be using any obviously illegal modifications such as noclip, show hitboxes, or speedhack
  5. Must not be using a clickbot such as "Click Sounds" (mod may be installed and enabled but must be inactive during your run, you WILL be required to show that the mod is inactive if you have it installed and enabled)
Note: If your video does not meet all of these requirements, your record may be rejected.

Submit Your Record

Before You Submit

Please note before submitting a level that your verification video must be uploaded to YouTube. Your video may be set to unlisted if you would like.

Level Requirements

  1. Must consist of no other game mode except wave
  2. Must not contain any crash triggers
  3. Must not be set to unlisted

Verification Requirements

  1. Must contain audible clicks, even on mobile
  2. Must contain your full unedited run, including your reaction and at least 3 attempts prior to your successful run
  3. Must show the end screen after level completion
  4. Must not be using any obviously illegal modifications such as noclip, show hitboxes, or speedhack
  5. Must not be using a clickbot such as "Click Sounds" (mod may be installed and enabled but must be inactive during your run, you WILL be required to show that the mod is inactive if you have it installed and enabled)
Note: The level must meet all of the level requirements AND the verification video must meet all of the verification requirements, otherwise the level may be rejected.
